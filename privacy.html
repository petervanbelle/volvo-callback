Privacy Policy

Application: Home Dashboard (private Node-RED dashboard) · Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who uses the application

The application is used only by its owner, for his own Volvo vehicle. There are no other users.

2. Which data

With the owner's consent through Volvo ID, the application reads vehicle data such as odometer, fuel or battery level, range, lock, door and window status, service information, warnings and, optionally, vehicle location.

3. Where data is stored

Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own Raspberry Pi on his home network. No data is stored on this website or on any other server.

4. Sharing

Data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

5. Retention and removal

The owner can delete the stored data at any time and can revoke the application's access through Volvo ID.
